Silver Cyanide

A chemical compound used in various industrial processes, including electroplating and the extraction of precious metals.

Product description

Silver cyanide, with the chemical formula AgCN, is a white or light-gray inorganic compound that appears as a fine crystalline powder, slightly soluble in water but soluble in alkaline cyanide solutions such as potassium cyanide.

It is widely used in galvanoplasty, especially in electrolytic silver baths, due to its ability to form soluble complexes of Silver

This compound must be handled with extreme caution due to the toxicity of the cyanide ion, so it is recommended only for industrial use under strict chemical safety protocols.

Aplicaciones

⚙️ Electroplating industry

Source of silver (Ag⁺) ions in the preparation of cyanide electrolytic baths, widely used in jewelry, electronic connectors, and decorative coatings.

🧪 Specialized chemical synthesis

It participates in the formation of silver metal complexes, catalysts, and precious metal recovery processes.

🔩 Manufacture of complex salts

Precursor to silver potassium cyanide (KAg(CN)₂) and other compounds used in electrodeposition processes.

🔬 Laboratories and research

In studies of noble metal reactivity and coordination chemistry.
