WELCOME TO VALOROS DE COLOMBIA

We are a company dedicated to the industrialization of precious metals, with advanced, environmentally sustainable production processes and a highly qualified human team. Our trajectory and recognition in the Colombian market are based on the quality of our products and services, the fulfillment of our sales promise, and excellent service.

GOLD LINE

At VALOROS DE COLOMBIA, we offer high-quality products in our Gold Line. Our certified products are ideal for various industrial and jewelry applications.

Pure Gold

24 karat precious metal, highly valued for its purity, shine, and resistance to corrosion. Ideal for jewelry, electronics, and investment.

Gold Wire

High-purity fine wire, used in detailed jewelry, microelectronics, and luxury embroidery. Maximum conductivity and strength.

SILVER LINE

At VALOROS DE COLOMBIA, we offer a wide range of high-quality products in our Silver Line. Our certified products are ideal for various industrial and jewelry applications.

Silver Nitrate

A chemical compound used in various industrial processes, electroplating, and precious metal extraction.

Silver Cyanide

High-purity silver wire, ideal for detailed jewelry and electronic applications.

Silver Potassium Cyanide

Used in various industrial processes, including electroplating and precious metal extraction.

Silver Anodes

Made of pure or alloyed silver.

Silver Wire

High-purity silver wire, ideal for detailed jewelry and electronic applications.

Pure Silver

Silver in bar, sheet, or granulated form with certified 99.99% purity.
